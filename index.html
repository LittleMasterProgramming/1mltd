About 1 Million Ltd.

A new future for Bangladesh.

Led by Founder and Chairman Muhammad Yeasir and Chief Executive Officer Safwaan Mustafa, the company is building a bridge between Bangladesh’s young talent and the opportunities emerging across the global digital economy.

Beyond training people to use technology.

We want to help create the conditions in which young Bangladeshis can build careers, deliver services to international clients, develop innovative solutions to national problems, and take part meaningfully in the global technology industry.

Our long-term goal is to empower one million people by 2028, develop one of Bangladesh’s largest freelancing and technology workforces, strengthen the country’s digital economy and help reduce unemployment through practical, marketable skills.

What we believe

The next generation should not have to leave to compete.

  • The next generation of Bangladeshi talent should compete internationally without leaving the country to join the global economy.
  • Technology should not be a privilege for a select few. It should be open to young people regardless of where they live or what they begin with.

  • With the right skills, opportunities, partnerships and determination, a million people can become a powerful force for national transformation.

Our connection with Google

Bringing Google closerto Bangladesh.

Google is central to our technology education strategy and long-term ambition: a catalyst for high-quality digital learning, cloud computing, AI, productivity tools, developer skills and globally recognised credentials.

A · Skills and certifications

Turning learning into capability.

Learners build their skills through Google’s educational resources and certification pathways. The approach combines Google and Meta certifications with practical experience, so young people gain real technical knowledge, not just certificates.

Access to the right learning resources helps narrow the gap between traditional education and the practical skills the global economy increasingly requires.

On average, each member of the Yeasirians core team holds about 100 Google and 20 Meta certifications.

B · Google Cloud Skills Boost

All ten of the global top ten.

Google Cloud Skills Boost is a practical learning environment for cloud technologies and related skills. Competition-based learning pushes participants to practise, show progress and build stronger technical portfolios.

Bangladeshi participants secured all ten places in the global top ten in the 2024 and 2025 competitions. We want to encourage many more young Bangladeshis to explore cloud computing, AI, data analytics, cybersecurity and other emerging fields.

These pillars describe the company’s strategic model and are not all fully operational services today.

Freelancing vision

From lone freelancers to coordinated teams.

1 Million Ltd. aspires to become one of Bangladesh’s largest freelancing and technology workforce platforms. Instead of every person learning skills, finding clients, negotiating contracts and managing everything alone, an ecosystem would let people build capability, collaborate and reach clients.

A business needing a complete digital marketing solution could work with one coordinated team rather than several unrelated freelancers. The model needs reliable quality assurance, project management, fair compensation, client acquisition and strong operations.

The ultimate ambition: help Bangladesh export digital services directly to the world, instead of exporting talent mainly through migration.

Strategy and roadmap

The road to one million.

Three years, one direction: build the foundation, expand the infrastructure, then scale.

2026

Foundation

Strengthen the training model, expand the 100 Projects initiative, improve team execution, develop learning pathways, document results, and build a credible case for engagement with Google and other partners.

2027

Expansion

Pursue the planned innovation hub, strengthen client relationships, develop collaborative digital service teams, and explore institutional and technology partnerships.

2028

Scale

Work toward empowering one million people: scale accessible skills development, expand paid digital work, grow the technology workforce and measure progress.

The timeline is our intended direction. Reaching the 2028 target will require scalable delivery, sustainable funding, strong partnerships and measurable employment outcomes.

Infrastructure

An innovation hub, and a platform for everyone.

A million-person ambition needs infrastructure that grows beyond a small training team. Our plans include an office and innovation hub in 2027, bringing together training, technology projects, workforce development and connections with international clients.

Alongside it, a scalable digital platform could offer learning resources, progress tracking, project collaboration, portfolios and pathways to work, eventually serving learners across Bangladesh. These are strategic plans, not services that are already operational.
